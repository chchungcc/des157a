rules

players will shake the jar to get more stars for their star score. the star dice above each player represents their two rolls from shaking the jar.

 first one to get a star score of 30 is the winner! 

if you roll a one, you don't get stars and the turn switches to the next player

beware! getting snake eyes (two ones) will reset your star score
